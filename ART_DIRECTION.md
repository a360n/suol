# Cinematic Art Direction

Original illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool, then copied into this project's images folder as optimized JPEG assets.

## images/abyss.jpg

Use case: illustration-story. Widescreen cinematic website background. A solitary young adult man with dark short hair seen from behind in a dark coat, standing on obsidian earth in a vast otherworldly abyss. A tiny brilliant electric blue soul orb floats in front of him, distant ember orange lava fissures, monumental shadows, fine fog, starless navy sky. Photorealistic movie concept art, anamorphic composition, realistic textures, restrained orange and blue, deep blacks, subject on right third, left side spacious dark negative space for titles. No text, logos, or watermarks.
